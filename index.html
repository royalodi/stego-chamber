📜 StegoChamber Security Protocol Specification & Architecture
OFFICIAL LAB DOCUMENTATION

1. Least Significant Bit (LSB) Theory

Digital images encode pixel channel intensities as 8-bit integers (0 to 255). The Least Significant Bit (Bit 0) contributes an intensity delta of at most 1 unit ($1/256 \approx 0.39\%$). Human retinal photoreceptors cannot discern this minute variation.

Pixel Original: 11010100 (212)
Embedded Bit: 1
Pixel Stego: 11010101 (213) [Delta: +1]

2. PRNG Noise Scatter (Crypto Dispersed)

Naive steganography writes sequential pixels from top-left to bottom-right, creating an unmistakable rectangular discontinuity detectable by Chi-Square analysis. StegoChamber features a seeded PRNG permutation engine that scatters bits across the carrier like uniform Gaussian noise.

Seed = SHA-256(Key || Salt)
Pixel Slot $S_i = \text{Permutation}(\text{Slots}, \text{Seed})[i]$
Result: Zero spatial clustering!

3. Authenticated AES-GCM-256 Packaging

All encrypted payloads undergo PBKDF2 key derivation with 100,000 SHA-256 iterations and a random 16-byte cryptographic salt. AES-GCM appends a 128-bit authentication tag, guaranteeing that any tampering or incorrect passphrase immediately trips an integrity failure.

4. 16-Bit Audio Steganography

Audio steganography manipulates the LSB of 16-bit signed PCM audio samples ($-32768$ to $+32767$). A $\pm 1$ delta represents $-96.3 \text{ dB}$ distortion, far beneath standard acoustic room noise and well past the threshold of human hearing acuity.

STG1 Binary Packet Specification:

[0..3] Magic: "STG1" (0x53544731)
[4] Flags: Bit 0=Encrypted, Bit 1=File, Bit 2=PRNG, Bits 3-4=Depth, Bits 5-6=Channels
[5..20] Salt: 16-Byte Cryptographic Salt
[21..32] IV: 12-Byte AES-GCM Initialization Vector
[33..36] Payload Length: Uint32 Big-Endian
[37..40] Checksum: Uint32 Big-Endian CRC-32
[41..N] Filename & MIME metadata (if File mode)
[N..M] Payload Ciphertext / Plaintext Data

6. Decoy Duress Architecture (Plausible Deniability)

Under coercive duress, an operator can provide the secondary Decoy Password. StegoChamber uses independently derived AES-GCM-256 keys to decrypt an innocent cover message (e.g. personal notes or shopping lists). To an external observer, the authentication tag validates completely, with zero cryptographic leakage of the primary secret vault's presence.

7. Cryptographic Tamper Seal (HMAC-SHA256)

A keyed HMAC-SHA256 signature is calculated over the image dimensions and raw RGBA pixel stream. The avalanche effect guarantees that any single-bit alteration, lossy recompression (JPEG/WebP), or geometric crop will completely invalidate the seal, triggering immediate visual and acoustic breach alerts.

8. Arbitrary RGBA Color Channel Bitmasks

StegoChamber encodes a 4-bit channel mask (R=1, G=2, B=4, A=8) into descriptor Byte 43. Operators can isolate embedding to specific chromatic planes (e.g., Red+Blue for dual-band stealth, or Alpha transparency for ghost steganography), with automated extraction decoding the exact channel layout from the header.

9. Ultrasonic & Acoustic Chime Carrier (Data-Over-Audio)

Frequency-Shift Keying (MFSK 16-ary) operates in the near-ultrasonic spectrum (18.2 kHz - 19.8 kHz), inaudible to human ears but captured by standard microphones. The live Web Audio receiver utilizes Goertzel filters and sliding FFT peak trackers to decode messages across air-gapped acoustic channels.
